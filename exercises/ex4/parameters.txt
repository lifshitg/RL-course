**Assignment 4: Hyperparameters & Observation Model Justification**

**1. Hyperparameters Configuration**

* **Particles (N = 500):** Provides a large enough pool to uniformly cover all free cells and withstand rejection-sampling depletion, while remaining small enough to guarantee fast, real-time belief updates.
* **UCB1 Exploration Constant (c = 1.0):** Given the binary/normalized reward structure (1.0 for a win), this value heavily weights the exploration term. It ensures the MCTS does not get trapped in local optima or settle for the first successful rollout it finds, forcing it to explore potentially shorter paths.
* **Rollout Max Depth (30):** Represents the ideal equilibrium for grid environments. It is deep enough for a random rollout policy to successfully hit a goal state, but shallow enough to execute thousands of simulations within the strict 1-second or 20-second time budgets.
* **Discount Factor ($\gamma = 0.99$):** Strongly prioritizes finding the most efficient, shortest paths to the goal while preventing the values of distant goals from vanishing completely.

**2. Observation Function Selection**

* **Choice:** Option B (Egocentric 3x3 Window).
* **Justification:** In a Box Pushing environment, symmetric spatial awareness is critical. An agent needs to know what is behind and beside it just as much as what is in front (e.g., assessing space to back up or align with a heavy box). Option A (Fixed North window) introduces a geographic bias, effectively blinding the agent to obstacles when moving South. The egocentric window eliminates this bias, yielding a uniform distribution of observations that allows the particle filter to rapidly and efficiently prune incorrect state hypotheses anywhere on the board.
